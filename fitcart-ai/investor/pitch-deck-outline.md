# Investor — Pitch Deck Outline (10 slides)

Talking points per slide. Keep it honest — the credibility of slides 6–9 comes from *not* over-claiming cart-sync.

---

## Slide 1 — Title / Vision
- **FitCart AI — Try before you buy, across every store.**
- One line: the AI fit + outfit intelligence layer between shoppers and fashion commerce.
- Founders, tagline, contact.

## Slide 2 — Problem
- Online fashion returns are ~25–40% (`UNVERIFIED`), driven by **size/fit + outfit uncertainty**.
- Shoppers juggle 5+ apps; can't visualize a full outfit on their own body.
- Costly for shoppers (money/time), brutal for retailer margins.

## Slide 3 — Solution
- Personalized avatar from photos → try on cross-store outfits → **Fit Score + Outfit Score** → buy at the store.
- Demo visual: avatar + 360° multi-angle + fit report.
- We answer *"will it fit me & does this look work?"* — not just *"how it looks."*

## Slide 4 — Why now
- Generative try-on crossed the quality bar (Google Doppl 2025, Walmart, Snap) — demand validated.
- Open models + hosted inference → startup-buildable.
- DPDP (2025) → trust moat opportunity.

## Slide 5 — Product / How it works
- 4 layers: Visualization · **Fit Intelligence** · **Outfit Intelligence** · Cross-store aggregation.
- Honest accuracy ladder (MVP→V3) + confidence scores.

## Slide 6 — Market
- India-first: large, young, mobile-first, return-plagued.
- TAM/SAM/SOM **framework with assumptions** (not a single fake number).
- Bottom-up: revenue/active user vs cost/active user.

## Slide 7 — Business model
- Affiliate (day 1) + Pro subscription + **B2B fit SDK** (V3).
- Unit-economics snapshot; path to positive contribution/user.

## Slide 8 — Competition & moat
- Positioning chart: high cross-store × high fit-intelligence = **empty quadrant we own**.
- **We don't beat Google on pixels; we win on fit-data + neutrality + India + B2B optionality.**
- The 4 moats (flywheel, neutrality, trust, SDK).

## Slide 9 — Honesty slide (differentiator) — *"What's hard, and our plan"*
- **Cart-sync isn't officially possible** → MVP ships compliant affiliate/deep-link; cart-sync is partnership-gated V3.
- Unit economics + AI quality = the risks we're engineering around.
- *This slide builds trust with sophisticated investors — most decks hide these.*

## Slide 10 — Roadmap, team & ask
- 90-day plan → pilot; V2/V3 vision.
- Team + key hires.
- **Ask:** pre-seed/seed for ~12–18 months to a monetizing MVP + first partnership.
- Use of funds: team, hosted AI, compliance, pilot.
